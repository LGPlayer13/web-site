no, really. who are you?

Guess I might as well share more. I'm some guy on the web who can't wrap his head around life. I'm still on this path of trying to reinvent myself, and give new meaning to my online identity. If I could go back in time, I would definitely change my username.

If you're wondering what 'LG' stands for, it might take a while for me to tell you. I have yet to redefine it.

My interests include gaming, sleeping, programming, walking, collecting stuff, and ranting. This site is the one place I feel "safe" being myself in, since I'm waaaay too shy to express myself in the real world. Could be that I'm too niche.

I also like music, mainly because it sounds good. If you ever saw me in the real world, I'd probably have an airpod in my ear. Here's a good tip for airpod wearers: turn the volume down. If you'd like to keep your ears from sporadically ringing like mine do, maybe dial back the loudness.

I think you reading this already gives you more information about me than most people would ever know. I'm a very reserved person, and I have a bad habit of avoiding all kinds of social interaction. ...I should probably start thinking about that.
