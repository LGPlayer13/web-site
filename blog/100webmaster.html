100 Questions for Webmasters

September 5, 2026

Template made by mouseling.net.

1. Please introduce yourself.
Good morning! I'm LG, and I make games (occasionally). I'm lazy as all hell, but moderately efficient when a computer's in front of me.
2. How long have you been making websites?
I started making this one halfway into my Freshman year of high school. After a good buddy of mine started showing off his own website, I felt very envious and had to make one myself. It looked a whole lot worse than his, but it's grown a lot since then.
3. And what got you into the hobby?
The friend I just mentioned. I also started taking a peek at other Neocities sites, and they gave me further motivation.
4. What kind of website are you most interested in?
Ones with blogs! I like reading things, even if I read too fast for my own good.
5. What's your workflow? Do you plan your websites out thoroughly or do you come up with the design as you go along?
I used to just make something up on the spot, which didn't usually result in the nicest of pages. Nowadays, I like to sketch what a page will look like in a notebook, along with writing down its contents. It's very helpful.
(i actually canned an entire update due to bad planning)
6. Please link to your biggest inspirations.
Nomnomnami, vulpecula, Wriorango, and kirbeedia.
I don't care for their code, it's all about the design.
7. What's your favourite part about making websites?
Getting to add some little bits of flavor to a page is my favorite part. If I actually knew how to draw, there'd be even more stuff.
8. And the thing you struggle with the most?
Actually, y'know, making it.
9. Do you keep the same layout on all of your pages? Or do you use different ones?
A majority of the pages have the same grid layout, but I like to branch out from time to time.
10. How confident are you with CSS?
I'd say I'm pretty decent at it. I've made some really neat stuff with it, and the mere fact that people have complimented it gives me confidence. Don't ask me to do colors, though.
11. Do you know how to correctly use <dl>?
Guess I do now.
12. What is your favourite HTML element?
Probably <span> or <p>. Both let me write fun things and add stupid blurbs to stuff.
13. If you're making a new web page from scratch, what is the first thing you do?
Sketch it out, figure out what it'll contain, and look at other pages that'll give me some inspiration.
14. Do you know JavaScript?
I know very little JS, but enough to make a few cool things. I did take a web design class in high school, but it didn't teach me jack shit.
15. How about PHP?
Nada.
16. Does your website have a theme that you stick to?
Not really, it varies quite a bit. I would say the checkerboard background kinda ties things together, but it's absent from a lot of pages (including this one).
17. Are you more focused on content or design?
I tend to focus more on design, but lately I'm trying to figure out a balance between the two. There's more than twenty pages on this site, I gotta fill 'em up with something.
18. Do you own a domain name? If not, would you ever want to?
Not at the moment, but it'd be cool. I think another LG Productions already exists, though.
19. What do you think of nostalgia-focused or "retro" websites?
They're neat, but could do with some more flavor. Don't base your entire site around having a "retro" design. Put some more of you in there.
20. Is your HTML valid? Do you even check?
Don't see any errors on my end, so it's probably fine.
21. What are your opinion on buttons and banners?
They're pretty cool, banners especially.
22. What do you think of button walls in particular?
I'm iffy on these. If you put a big ass button wall on the front page of your site, it's an eyesore for me. Put that kind of thing in its own page.
23. If you started over again, would you make something similar or completely different?
I'd likely stick with what I've got right now.
24. Are you envious of other people's websites?
Somewhat, but I'm learning to appreciate what I can do.
25. What text editor do you use?
VSCodium.
26. Why do you use that one?
It's responsive, removes all the telemetry of VSCode, has a live preview extension, and doesn't force AI down your throat like some other editors out there.
27. Do you host your image files on your web server, or on another host?
For the website? I use Neocities. For my games? I use GitHub and File Garden.
28. This might not be relevant to you, but what's your opinion on the Neocities vs. Nekoweb debate?
I have little to say about it. But from what I've heard, Neocities is the much better choice.
29. How much server space would you estimate your main website takes up?
I doubt it takes much at all. Probably like, 20 MB.
30. Do you keep local backups of your files?
Yes.
31. Do you prefer simple or highly visual websites?
Simple, clean websites are what get my attention. Highly visual websites are cool too, as long as they're actually legible.
32. Do you stick to certain colours? Do you do that on purpose, or is it your subconscious?
My "primary" colors are indigo and light pink, but I don't stick to them. I like giving every page its own color palette.
33. Have you ever thought about quitting? Why?
As much fun as being even lazier sounds, I wouldn't know how else to express myself on the web. I kinda need this web site.
34. Do you have many webmaster friends, or is it a solitary hobby?
I know a few, and I should probably get my other friends to make sites of their own.
35. Do people in your real life know about your website?
No god please no do you know how difficult it'd be to have to explain half the shit i've written in the blog god i'd rather not
36. Do you update your website very often? How often is "very often"?
I've noticed a pattern where I release major updates around six months apart. Well, I should say released, because the latest update (v5.0) took a tad longer.
37. And the overall design, do you change that much? Why or why not?
Oh, it changes a LOT with every update. Each one could probably be its own site.
38. Is your website more you-focused, hobby-focused, or outside world-focused?
Definitely more hobby-focused. I don't think I'm terribly interesting as a person, so I prefer to rant about the stuff I've made.
39. Do you do web design professionally?
Nope.
40. If not, would you like to? And if you're comfortable answering, what do you do for work?
Still no. And I don't even have a job to begin with.
41. Do you communicate with people by email very much?
No, all the kids these days use their insta grams and diss cords.
42. Some people reject social media and use websites as a replacement. Do you keep social media outside of your website?
Yes. Having the few that I use out on display would feel odd. Like, what could I possibly achieve by having my Reddit account here.
43. How about instant messengers? Do you use a mainstream one like Discord or Telegram? Or something like Matrix? Do you avoid them?
Discord, because that's what everyone uses.
44. Do you listen to music while you work on websites? If so, what kinds of artists?
I listen to music while doing literally anything. I'm into a lot of indie stuff.
45. Do you keep everything you make on one website, or do you have more than one?
It's all here, having it scattered about would be incredibly inconvenient.
46. On a similar note, do you keep to one topic on your site, or many?
I talk about a variety of things.
47. Do you present your real self, or at least try? Or do you construct a persona on purpose?
This site is all me. My interests, personality, creations, depressive episodes; it's all here for your viewing pleasure.
48. Have you ever made a good friend thanks to your website?
Nope.
49. Are you happy with the way HTML and CSS currently work?
CSS is kind of a pain in the ass at times, but it's fine. Same with HTML. Actually, they're both kind of a pain. But doesn't that apply to every language, in one way or another?
50. What are practices that you think people should avoid?
Flashing images, comically small text, oversaturated colors, disabling right-clicks, autoplaying music, and using <div>'s for everything. Autoplaying music is especially annoying for someone who always listens to his own music.
51. What about under-utilised practices, or things you think people should do more?
Hell if I know. I think most people have got it down.
52. Do you use a lot of semantic HTML? Or are you guilty of generic structure?
I try my best. You can never avoid using <div>, but I use any semantics when I get the chance.
53. Do you consider different browsers?
I stick to one browser most of the time. I also have Firefox, but I only use it to make sure it doesn't break my web site.
54. Speaking of, what's your preferred browser? Convince your readers why they should use it.
My browser of choice is Brave, because of its build-in adblocker. I know a lot of people complain about the Web3 stuff it has, but you can just ignore it.
55. And what OS are you on?
CachyOS! Yes, I'm a Linux user. Windows 11 was a mess when I left, and it probably still is now.
56. Do you have a strong opinion on that, or do you just happen to use it?
Not necessarily. CachyOS runs well, it supports everything I need, and it's not Windows. You should move to Linux. It's good for you.
57. Are your websites mobile-friendly?
I don't think I've ever checked. That's probably a bad sign.
58. What are your thoughts on autoplay?
Annoying.
59. What are your thoughts on webrings? Are you in any?
They're cool, but I'm not a part of any.
60. Do you have any web shrines? What do you like to see in that sort of page?
Yeah, I've got one. Put some cool decor, like make the whole page themed around that subject with cool decor, images, etc. And make sure to dump a ton of info and history that no one cares about.
61. Are your websites "cliche", in your opinion?
I don't think so? If by "cliche" you mean "have the same stuff", then I guess so. But I think most sites could do with a guestbook, links page, and all that stuff.
62. What is your ideal website? Are you striving for that, or for something else?
One that makes sense, and doesn't suck complete booty cheeks. It should also feel personal and full of heart, not something you just vibe-coded in a half hour.
63. Are you an artist? Do you draw or design your own assets?
I pull some assets from other websites (mainly some background decor), and I make sure they're free to use. I make the rest myself, but I would NOT call myself an artist.
64. What are your favourite resource sites?
I use Vecteezy for some things, but I wouldn't call it a favorite. Please recommend some better sites for me to find stuff!
65. Is there a habit you just can't get away from no matter how hard you try?
Being overly self-deprecating. And taking too much inspiration from other sites.
66. What's your biggest advice for a new webmaster?
Don't do the things I just listed.
67. Do you keep all your styling in CSS? Or do you hard-code some?
The big stuff is lumped into one big file, but there's still a ton of hard-coded stuff.
68. What do you think of frameset layouts?
They exist. (but what are they)
69. How about table-based layouts?
They exist.
70. Do you subscribe to the ideas of "one-column", "two-column" and "three-column" layouts? Do you use any of these?
My site technically has three-column layout, but I'll usually only have up to two articles per row. And some pages get rid of the layout entirely.
71. Do you spend longer on the HTML or the CSS?
CSS, easily. I can just copy-paste the HTML from other pages, while the CSS has much more involvement and requires a lot more patience.
72. Have you ever made a page with no CSS? It's useful for your thoughts.
I made one once out of extreme laziness. It had a heading tag with five words in it.
73. Do you ever find yourself making layouts with nothing to put on them? Or do you only make layouts when the need arises?
My layouts always have something, but there are certains parts I struggle to fill.
74. Would you consider yourself a beginner? Or advanced? Somewhere in the middle?
We actually have a word for the middle! It's intermediate.
75. Do you have a habit of looking at the source code of websites you visit?
Nah, I can write code myself.
76. How did YOU learn how to make websites?
A lot of W3Schools. Though, I brought over a lot of CSS experience I had with Idle Game Maker back when I started.
77. Do you ever force elements to do things they're not supposed to?
That only amounts to me changing something's display from block to inline-block.
78. Thoughts on floating elements?
They're a lot of fun! Just make sure they don't get in the way of the content. And make sure to test them when zooming in and out!
(i'm assuming you mean elements that are literally floating, right, not the float property)
79. When you're sizing stuff, what do you use first? Do you use px, em, %, or something else?
I use px for sizing. The other two are reserved for padding and moving stuff around, respectively.
80. Do you have a favourite font?
Varela Round, the font this is written in.
81. Would you run a website with another person? How would that work?
I love my friends, but I'm keeping web dev to myself. I need all the creative control I can get.
82. Do you surf the Web to find new personal websites very often?
Wish I did it more often, but it's always fun to browse Neocities. Most of the sites I have in the Links page were from me looking around.
83. Do you bookmark other people's websites? How would you feel knowing someone else bookmarked yours?
No, I look through my search history. Though, if someone bookmarked my site, I'd be flattered.
84. What do you want people to be most impressed with when they see your website?
How much better it looks in comparison to how the site looked before v5.0.
85. Are you interested in technology outside of websites? Do you collect?
Hell yeah! I collect consoles, specifically handhelds. My favorite is my Game Boy Micro.
This is probably why I'm too niche for most people...
86. How often and for how long are you online?
On weekends, I'm online basically all day. On weekdays, I'm usually busy until 5:00. I actually have a life outside of the internet, which still surprises me.
87. When it comes to your website, who is your target audience?
My web site is technically PG-13, but I don't care if little Timmy goes snooping around on his dad's work laptop. What happens in the blog stays in the blog, however.
88. Have you ever been interested in XHTML?
What is that
89. Do you program in general? Have you ever written a program for use with or on your website, not counting simple JavaScript?
I make games on Scratch, if you count that as programming. I've also written some extremely basic JavaScript for the site.
90. Speaking of programs that help you make websites, what do you think of static site generators (SSGs)? Have you ever used one?
I can barely wrap my head around what an SSG even is.
91. Do you keep a hitcounter? Why or why not?
No, because I think my adblocker has blocked all the good ones.
92. Do you frequent forums? Which ones?
If you count the forum channels on Discord, then sure!
93. Do you write your page content directly into the editor, or do you prepare it elsewhere, like a text document or a Word document?
While I tried to start writing blog posts in a Google Doc, it became too much of a hassle having to copy and paste every. Single. Line. Of Text. Not to mention having to divide the text into my little sections, and then counting all the words after that... so my answer is no.
94. Do you think you appear cool to others? A more accurate answer now: do other people ever say you're cool?
I prefer to think of myself as a stupid idiot loser. It's pretty cool.
95. Are you embarrassed of your old work? Have you ever deleted everything out of shame?
I am embarrassed by a lot of my old crap, but I'd never delete it. Everything gets tossed into the archive. Very few things are left in the vault.
96. Would you close down your website if you couldn't update it, or would you leave an archive?
The only scenario I could think of is if I died, in which case it'd probably be too late to close the site. Who knows, my internet could shut down and never return. But then again, I could just stop by my local Starbucks. Ah, but I probably wouldn't have the files for my site anymore. I could just download them off GitHub, but the network speed would probably be too slow. Wait, who am I kidding? This site is like, 20 megabytes. But my laptop is slow as shit!! I could never get anything done on that old hunk of junk. I'd have to invest in some fancy new laptop, maybe one of those tablet hybrid things. But it looks like my internet is still working, so I probably won't have to worry about any of that.
97. Do you reveal a lot about yourself on your website? Or are you more secretive?
I've revealed a lot more about myself than I probably should. This site is a breeding ground for useless trivia.
98. Are you willing to reveal who your best online friend is, and/or if they have a website?
My good buddy, hyphen-lost. He's the guy I mentioned about 96 questions ago.
99. And do you optimise the images on your website?
I use this handy tool called optiPNG.
100. We're out of time! How do you feel after answering 100 questions? ....other than exhausted.
I feel like I've just spent three hours of my life answering a lot of questions I barely knew how to comprehend. A day well spent.
