Official Devlog

overviewlast updated on 10/01/26

Welcome! This is the official devlog for George 1.5, the half-sequel to George the Jumping Triangle. What started out as a series of (crappy) beta builds has turned into a game with an actual premise and good gameplay. If you're wondering what led up to George 1.5, check out the links below.

  • George 1 Remastered
  • George 2 [OLD DEMO]
  • George 2: Redefined Demo

Currently, there are no plans for any early access or demo versions of George 1.5. Any and all future updates will be posted here, in the section below.

More about the game (spoilers!)

George 1.5 serves as an "in-between" chapter, connecting the (abrupt) ending of George 1 to the future events of George 2. It focuses on the relationship between George and Greg, and how George has to cope with losing the last remaining member of his family.

The game will take place across five levels, with cutscenes and boss fights between each one. You be ranked based on your performance in each level, from E to S+ tier. The game can be saved after each level, recording your times and ranks.
