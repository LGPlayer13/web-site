the graphics

  • GIMP - this is what i use for basically everything image-related.
  • Turbowarp - yes, i make art in Scratch. isn't my life sad?
  • Aseprite - excellent pixel art tool that allows for animations, gradients, and more!
  • Inkscape - the vector editor i should probably be using.

  • Vecteezy - okay so this one is actually kinda bad, but it is generally reliable for transparent PNGs. just make sure to filter out the AI slop.
